# 📘 TIME & WORK – HARD LEVEL CONCEPT


## 🔴 TYPE 1: FRACTION + PARTIAL WORK (TRICKY)

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* “A does 1/3 work in 5 days…”
* “Work half done → then B joins”

### 📌 எப்படி solve பண்ணணும்:

👉 Always convert to **total work = LCM**
👉 Split work into stages

---

## 🔴 TYPE 2: MULTIPLE PERSON JOIN/LEAVE

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* “A works 5 days, then B joins”
* “C leaves after few days”

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Step-wise work calculation
👉 Remaining work method

---

## 🔴 TYPE 3: EFFICIENCY COMPARISON

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* “A is 3 times efficient than B”
* “A & B efficiency ratio”

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Efficiency ratio = inverse of time
👉 Use unit method

---

## 🔴 TYPE 4: PIPE & CISTERN (NET WORK)

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* Fill + empty together

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Net work = fill – empty
👉 Negative value → empty

---

## 🔴 TYPE 5: ALTERNATE WORK (VERY TRICKY)

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* A works 1 day, B next day

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Find 2-day cycle
👉 Multiply cycles

---

## 🔴 TYPE 6: WAGES (WORK BASED)

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* Money division based on work

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Wage ∝ work done

---

## 🔴 TYPE 7: WORK + TIME CHANGE

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* Work stops midway
* Worker removed

### 📌 எப்படி solve:

👉 Work done + remaining

---

## 🔴 TYPE 8: MIXED COMBINATION (TNPSC FAVORITE 🔥)

👉 Combine:
✔ Efficiency
✔ Join/leave
✔ Partial work

---

# ✍️ HARD LEVEL SOLVED EXAMPLES (10)

---

## 🧠 Example 1 (Join after partial)

A alone can do in 12 days
After 4 days, B joins → finish in 4 more days

👉 Work in 4 days = 4/12 = 1/3
👉 Remaining = 2/3

👉 A+B in 4 days = 2/3
👉 1 day = 1/6

👉 A = 1/12
👉 B = 1/6 - 1/12 = 1/12

✔ B alone = 12 days

---

## 🧠 Example 2 (Efficiency)

A is 3 times efficient than B
B takes 18 days

👉 A time = 18/3 = 6 days

---

## 🧠 Example 3 (Pipe tricky)

Pipe A fills in 6 hrs
Pipe B empties in 8 hrs

👉 Net = 1/6 - 1/8
👉 = (4-3)/24 = 1/24

✔ Tank fills in 24 hrs

---

## 🧠 Example 4 (Alternate)

A = 10 days, B = 15 days

👉 2 days work = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6

👉 6 days → 3 cycles = 1/2

👉 Continue → total = 8 days

---

## 🧠 Example 5 (Wages)

A does 2/3 work, B does 1/3
Total money = 900

👉 Ratio = 2:1
👉 A = 600, B = 300

---

## 🧠 Example 6 (Join + leave)

A = 10 days, B = 20 days

👉 A works 5 days → 5/10 = 1/2
👉 Remaining = 1/2

👉 B does → 1/2 ÷ (1/20) = 10 days

✔ Total = 15 days

---

## 🧠 Example 7 (Efficiency ratio)

A:B = 2:3
Total time together = 6 days

👉 Total efficiency = 5 units

👉 Work = 30

👉 A time = 30/2 = 15

---

## 🧠 Example 8 (Pipe + leak)

Fill = 5 hrs
Leak = 10 hrs

👉 Net = 1/5 - 1/10 = 1/10

✔ Time = 10 hrs

---

## 🧠 Example 9 (Work stop)

A = 12 days

👉 Works 6 days → 1/2 done
👉 Stops

👉 Remaining = 1/2

👉 B = 6 days

✔ Total = 12 days

---

## 🧠 Example 10 (Mixed hardest)

A = 12 days, B = 18 days

👉 Together = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36

👉 Work 4 days = 20/36

👉 Remaining = 16/36

👉 B alone → time = (16/36) ÷ (1/18)
👉 = 8 days

✔ Total = 12 days
