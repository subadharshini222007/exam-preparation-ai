
# 📘 PROFIT & LOSS – COMPLETE MODULE (TNPSC GROUP 1)

---

# 🟢 1. BASIC CONCEPT (BUT EXAM LEVEL)

👉 Cost Price (CP) = வாங்கிய விலை
👉 Selling Price (SP) = விற்ற விலை

✔ Profit = SP – CP
✔ Loss = CP – SP

---

### 📌 Important:

👉 Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
👉 Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100

👉 SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100
👉 SP = CP × (100 – Loss%) / 100

---

# 🔥 2. TYPES (ONLY HARD LEVEL VIEW)

---

## 🔴 TYPE 1: SUCCESSIVE PROFIT / LOSS

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* 20% profit, then 10% loss

### 📌 Formula:

👉 Net % = a + b + (ab/100)

---

## 🔴 TYPE 2: MARKED PRICE + DISCOUNT

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* Marked price, discount, profit

### 📌 Formula:

👉 SP = MP × (100 – discount%) / 100

---

## 🔴 TYPE 3: FALSE WEIGHT (VERY IMPORTANT 🔥)

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க:

* 1kg instead of 900g

### 📌 Trick:

👉 Profit % = (Error / Actual) × 100

---

## 🔴 TYPE 4: MIXED PROFIT + DISCOUNT

👉 Combine discount + profit

---

## 🔴 TYPE 5: COST CHANGE + SAME SP

👉 CP change → profit change

---

## 🔴 TYPE 6: SELLING SAME ITEM DIFFERENT PRICES

👉 Gain + loss → net result

---

## 🔴 TYPE 7: PART SELLING (VERY TRICKY)

👉 Half profit, half loss

---

## 🔴 TYPE 8: MULTIPLE CONDITIONS (TNPSC FAVORITE 🔥)

👉 Combine 2–3 concepts

# 🧠 3. HARD LEVEL SOLVING TRICKS

✔ Always assume CP = 100 (shortcut)
✔ Convert % → fraction
✔ Use net formula for successive
✔ Avoid long calculation

---

# ✍️ 4. HARD LEVEL SOLVED EXAMPLES (10)

---

## 🧠 Example 1 (Successive)

20% profit, then 10% loss

👉 Net = 20 - 10 - (20×10)/100
👉 = 20 -10 -2 = 8% profit

✔ Answer = 8% profit

---

## 🧠 Example 2 (Marked Price)

MP = 1000, discount = 20%
CP = 700

👉 SP = 800
👉 Profit = 100

👉 % = (100/700)×100 = 14.28%

👉 Profit = 100g

Loss 10% twice

👉 Net = 10 + 10 - (10×10)/100
👉 = 19% loss

# 📘 TYPE 1: SUCCESSIVE PROFIT & LOSS

---

## 🧠 CORE IDEA (IMPORTANT)

👉 இரண்டு percentage changes வந்தா:

✔ Net % = a + b + (a×b)/100  *(both profit)*
✔ Net % = a - b - (a×b)/100 *(profit + loss)*

👉 Shortcut:
✔ Same % profit & loss → **always loss**

---

# ✍️ 10 HARD LEVEL SOLVED EXAMPLES

---
