# 🟢 1. BASIC CONTENT (CONCEPT)

Ratio என்பது இரண்டு quantities-ஐ compare பண்ணுறது.
👉 a : b = a/b

Proportion என்பது இரண்டு ratios equal ஆக இருக்குறது.
👉 a : b = c : d
👉 a/b = c/d

👉 Important:
a × d = b × c

---

# 🟡 2. TYPES OF QUESTIONS (VERY IMPORTANT)

---

## 🔹 TYPE 1: SIMPLIFY RATIO

### 📌 Concept

Ratio-ஐ smallest form-க்கு reduce பண்ணுவது

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* Simplify 20:30
* Reduce 45:60

---

## 🔹 TYPE 2: FIND MISSING VALUE

### 📌 Concept

Unknown value find using proportion

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* 2:3 = x:12
* 5:7 = 10:x

---

## 🔹 TYPE 3: DIVISION OF QUANTITY

### 📌 Concept

Total amount-ஐ ratio-ப்படி divide பண்ணணும்

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* Divide 100 in ratio 2:3
* Divide ₹600 in 1:2:3

---

## 🔹 TYPE 4: DIRECT PROPORTION

### 📌 Concept

One increases → other also increases

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* Cost vs quantity
* Work vs wages

---

## 🔹 TYPE 5: INVERSE PROPORTION

### 📌 Concept

One increases → other decreases

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* Workers vs days
* Speed vs time

---

## 🔹 TYPE 6: COMBINED RATIO

### 📌 Concept

Two ratios combine பண்ணி final ratio கண்டுபிடிக்கணும்

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* a:b, b:c given → find a:c or a:b:c

---

## 🔹 TYPE 7: AGE PROBLEMS

### 📌 Concept

Present + future/past relation

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* “after 5 years”
* “before 3 years”

---

## 🔹 TYPE 8: MIXTURE PROBLEMS

### 📌 Concept

Milk & water type ratio change

### 📌 எப்படி கேப்பாங்க

* After adding/removing liquid
* New ratio

---

👉 Step 3: Use cross multiplication

### 🧠 Example 1 (Simplify)

Simplify 30 : 45

👉 Divide by 15
👉 2 : 3

✔ Answer = 2 : 3

---

### 🧠 Example 2 (Simplify)

Simplify 48 : 60

👉 Divide by 12
👉 4 : 5

✔ Answer = 4 : 5

---

### 🧠 Example 3 (Missing Value)

2 : 3 = x : 15

👉 2/3 = x/15
👉 2×15 = 3x
👉 30 = 3x
👉 x = 10

✔ Answer = 10

---

### 🧠 Example 4 (Missing Value)

5 : 8 = 20 : x

👉 5/8 = 20/x
👉 5x = 160
👉 x = 32

✔ Answer = 32

---

### 🧠 Example 5 (Division)

Divide 150 in ratio 2 : 3

👉 Total parts = 5
👉 150/5 = 30

👉 Values:
2×30 = 60
3×30 = 90

✔ Answer = 60, 90

---

### 🧠 Example 6 (Division)

Divide ₹900 in 1 : 2 : 3

👉 Total = 6 parts
👉 900/6 = 150

👉 Values:
1×150 = 150
2×150 = 300
3×150 = 450

✔ Answer = 150, 300, 450

---

### 🧠 Example 7 (Direct Proportion)

3 pens cost ₹60. Cost of 5 pens?

👉 Direct proportion

👉 x = (60×5)/3 = 100

✔ Answer = ₹100

---

### 🧠 Example 8 (Inverse Proportion)

6 workers complete work in 8 days
How many days for 12 workers?

👉 6×8 = 12×x
👉 48 = 12x
👉 x = 4

✔ Answer = 4 days

---

### 🧠 Example 9 (Combined Ratio)

a:b = 3:4
b:c = 5:6

👉 LCM of 4 & 5 = 20

👉 3:4 ×5 = 15:20
👉 5:6 ×4 = 20:24

👉 a:b:c = 15:20:24

✔ Answer = 15:20:24

---

### 🧠 Example 10 (Age Problem)

Ratio = 2:3
After 4 years = 3:4

👉 Ages = 2x, 3x

(2x+4)/(3x+4) = 3/4

👉 4(2x+4) = 3(3x+4)
👉 8x+16 = 9x+12
👉 x = 4

👉 Ages = 8, 12

✔ Answer = 8 & 12

# 📘 HARD LEVEL SOLVED EXAMPLES

---

## 🧠 Example 1 (Combined + Fraction Ratio)

If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, find (a + b) : (b + c)

👉 Step 1: Make b common
LCM(3,4) = 12

👉 a:b = 2:3 → 8:12
👉 b:c = 4:5 → 12:15

👉 a:b:c = 8:12:15

👉 (a+b) : (b+c)
= (8+12) : (12+15)
= 20 : 27

✔ Answer = 20 : 27

---

## 🧠 Example 2 (Age – Advanced)

Present ratio = 3 : 5
After 10 years = 5 : 7

👉 Let ages = 3x, 5x

(3x+10)/(5x+10) = 5/7

👉 7(3x+10) = 5(5x+10)
👉 21x + 70 = 25x + 50
👉 20 = 4x
👉 x = 5

👉 Ages = 15, 25

✔ Answer = 15 & 25

---

## 🧠 Example 3 (Mixture – Tricky)

Milk : Water = 4 : 1
10L mixture removed and water added → new ratio 4 : 3

👉 Let initial = 4x, x → total = 5x

Removed 10L → remove in same ratio
Milk removed = 8L
Water removed = 2L

👉 Remaining:
Milk = 4x - 8
Water = x - 2

Add 10L water:
Water = x - 2 + 10 = x + 8

👉 New ratio:
(4x - 8)/(x + 8) = 4/3

👉 3(4x - 8) = 4(x + 8)
👉 12x - 24 = 4x + 32
👉 8x = 56
👉 x = 7

✔ Total = 35L

---

## 🧠 Example 4 (Income & Saving)

Income ratio = 4 : 5
Expenditure equal
A saves ₹200

👉 Let income = 4x, 5x
Let expenditure = y

👉 A saving = 4x - y = 200
👉 B saving = 5x - y

👉 Subtract:
(5x - y) - (4x - y) = x

👉 So B saving = 200 + x

But no y → assume:
x = 200

👉 B saving = 400

✔ Answer = ₹400

---

## 🧠 Example 5 (Work + Ratio)

8 men can do work in 12 days
Men : Women efficiency = 2 : 1

How many women needed to do in 12 days?

👉 Work = 8 × 12 = 96 units

Men efficiency = 2
Women = 1

👉 Total work by women:
x × 12 × 1 = 96

👉 12x = 96
👉 x = 8

✔ Answer = 8 women

---

## 🧠 Example 6 (Difference Based)

Ratio = 7 : 9
Difference = 40

👉 2 parts = 40
👉 1 part = 20

👉 Numbers:
7×20 = 140
9×20 = 180

✔ Answer = 140, 180

---

## 🧠 Example 7 (Fraction Ratio Conversion)

A : B = 2/3 : 4/5

👉 Convert to whole numbers

LCM = 15

👉 2/3 ×15 = 10
👉 4/5 ×15 = 12

✔ Ratio = 10 : 12 = 5 : 6

---

## 🧠 Example 8 (Chain Ratio)

A : B = 3 : 4
B : C = 5 : 6
C : D = 2 : 3

👉 Make common chain

👉 A:B = 3:4
B:C = 5:6 → multiply first by 5 → 15:20
→ B:C = 20:24

👉 C:D = 2:3 → multiply by 12 → 24:36

👉 Final:
A:B:C:D = 15:20:24:36

---

## 🧠 Example 9 (Mixture Reverse)

Milk:Water = 3:2
After adding 10L water → 3:4

👉 3x : 2x + 10 = 3 : 4

👉 4×3x = 3(2x + 10)
👉 12x = 6x + 30
👉 6x = 30
👉 x = 5

✔ Total = 25L

---

## 🧠 Example 10 (Double Condition)

A:B = 2:3
B:C = 3:4
C:D = 4:5

Find A:D

👉 Combine:

A:B = 2:3
B:C = 3:4
C:D = 4:5

👉 Direct chain:
A:D = 2:5

✔ Answer = 2 : 5
