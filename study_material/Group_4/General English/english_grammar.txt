# 📄  INTRODUCTION

Grammar என்பது ஒரு மொழியின் கட்டமைப்பு. English grammar helps us to speak, write and understand English correctly. It is essential for competitive exams like TNPSC.

Grammar is divided into parts such as Parts of Speech, Tenses, Voice, Direct & Indirect speech, Articles, Prepositions and Vocabulary.

---

# 📄 PARTS OF SPEECH 

Words in English are divided into 8 parts of speech:

1. Noun
2. Pronoun
3. Verb
4. Adjective
5. Adverb
6. Preposition
7. Conjunction
8. Interjection

Each word in a sentence belongs to one of these categories.

Example:
Ram is a good boy
Ram = Noun, is = Verb, good = Adjective

---

# 📄  NOUN

A noun is a naming word. It refers to a person, place, animal or thing.

Types of nouns:

* Proper noun: Chennai, Ram
* Common noun: city, boy
* Collective noun: team, army
* Abstract noun: honesty, beauty

Example:
Honesty is the best policy.

---

# 📄  PRONOUN

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Types:

* Personal: he, she, they
* Reflexive: himself, herself
* Possessive: his, her

Example:
Ram is a good boy. He is honest.

---

# 📄  VERB

A verb is an action word.

Examples:

* go, come, write, eat

Forms:

* go – went – gone
* write – wrote – written

Example:
She writes a letter.

---

# 📄 ADJECTIVE

An adjective describes a noun.

Examples:

* beautiful girl
* big house
* good boy

Example:
She is a beautiful girl.

---

# 📄 ADVERB

An adverb describes a verb.

Examples:

* quickly
* slowly
* carefully

Example:
He runs quickly.

---

# 📄 PREPOSITION

A preposition shows relation between words.

Examples:

* in
* on
* at
* under

Example:
The book is on the table.

---

# 📄 CONJUNCTION

A conjunction joins words or sentences.

Examples:

* and
* but
* or

Example:
I like tea and coffee.

---

# 📄 INTERJECTION

Interjection shows sudden emotion.

Examples:

* Oh!
* Wow!
* Alas!

Example:
Wow! That is beautiful.

---

# 📄  TENSE INTRO

Tense shows time of action.

Types:

* Present
* Past
* Future

Example:
I eat (present)
I ate (past)
I will eat (future)

---

# 📄  SIMPLE PRESENT

Used for habitual actions.

Examples:

* He goes to school daily
* She reads books

---

# 📄 PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Action happening now.

Examples:

* He is reading
* She is writing

---

# 📄  PRESENT PERFECT

Completed action with result.

Examples:

* He has finished his work
* She has eaten food

---

# 📄 PAST TENSE

Completed actions.

Examples:

* He went to school
* She wrote a letter

---

# 📄 FUTURE TENSE

Future actions.

Examples:

* He will go
* She will come

---

# 📄  ACTIVE VOICE

Subject performs action.

Example:
Ram eats apple.

---

# 📄  PASSIVE VOICE

Object becomes subject.

Example:
Apple is eaten by Ram.

---

# 📄  DIRECT SPEECH

Exact words.

Example:
He said, “I am happy.”

---

# 📄  INDIRECT SPEECH

Reported speech.

Example:
He said that he was happy.

---

# 📄 PAGE 70–73: ARTICLES

* a → consonant sound
* an → vowel sound
* the → specific

Example:
a book, an apple, the sun

---

# 📄  PREPOSITIONS ADVANCED

Examples:

* in the room
* on the table
* at 5 o’clock

---

# 📄  SYNONYMS

Words with same meaning:

happy = joyful
big = large
small = tiny

---

# 📄 ANTONYMS

Opposite meaning:

hot ≠ cold
fast ≠ slow

---

# 📄  IDIOMS

Break the ice = start conversation
Hit the books = study hard

---

# 📄  PHRASES

* take care of
* look after
* give up

---

#  ONE WORD SUBSTITUTION

* One who writes books = Author
* One who treats patients = Doctor

---

# 📄 SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Subject + Verb + Object

Example:
Ram eats rice.

---

# 📄 DEGREE COMPARISON

* big
* bigger
* biggest

---

# 📄 COMMON ERRORS

He go ❌ → He goes ✔
She are ❌ → She is ✔

# 📄  QUESTION TAGS

# 📄  PREFIX & SUFFIX

**Punctuation Marks - Rules & Examples 

**1. Full Stop / Period ( . )**
- Used at the end of a statement.
- Example: I am going to school.
- Example: Chennai is the capital of Tamil Nadu.

**2. Comma ( , )**
- Used to separate items in a list, clauses, or add pause.
- Example: I bought apples, oranges, and bananas.
- Example: After finishing his work, he went home.

**3. Question Mark ( ? )**
- Used at the end of a direct question.
- Example: What is your name?
- Example: Where do you live?

**4. Exclamation Mark ( ! )**
- Used to express strong emotion.
- Example: What a beautiful sunset!
- Example: Wow! You won the prize!

**5. Apostrophe ( ' )**
- Used for possession or contraction.
- Example: This is Ravi's book. (possession)
- Example: I don't know. (contraction of "do not")

**6. Quotation Marks ( " " )**
- Used to indicate direct speech or a quote.
- Example: She said, "I will come tomorrow."
- Example: The teacher said, "Study well for the exam."

**7. Colon ( : )**
- Used before a list, explanation, or example.
- Example: There are three primary colours: red, blue, and yellow.
- Example: He had one goal: to win.

**8. Semicolon ( ; )**
- Used to join two related independent clauses.
- Example: I have a test tomorrow; I need to study.
- Example: She likes tea; he prefers coffee.

**9. Hyphen ( - )**
- Used to join compound words or split words at a line break.
- Example: mother-in-law
- Example: well-known author

**10. Dash ( — )**
- Used to show a break in thought or add emphasis.
- Example: I was going to call you — but I forgot.
- Example: Everyone was there — except Ravi.

**11. Brackets/Parentheses ( )**
- Used to add extra information.
- Example: Mahatma Gandhi (1869–1948) was born in Gujarat.
- Example: He finally answered (after a long pause).

**12. Capital Letters**
- Used for the first word of a sentence, proper nouns, and "I".
- Example: Ravi lives in Chennai.
- Example: I am learning English.

**TNPSC Exam Tips:**
- "Punctuate the following sentence" type questions frequently appear
- Direct/Indirect speech questions test quotation marks and comma usage heavily
- Apostrophe (possession vs contraction) is a common confusion point tested
