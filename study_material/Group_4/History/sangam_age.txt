
# 🏺 SANGAM AGE – TAMIL HISTORY 

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION TO SANGAM AGE

Sangam Age refers to the period of ancient Tamil history between approximately 300 BCE to 300 CE. It is considered the golden age of Tamil literature and culture. The term “Sangam” means an assembly or academy of Tamil poets held under the patronage of Pandya kings in Madurai.

This period is important in TNPSC exams because many questions come from Sangam literature, kingdoms, poets, and geography of ancient Tamilakam.

---

## 2. THREE SANGAMS (THIRU SANGAM)

According to Tamil tradition, there were three Sangams:

### First Sangam

* Location: Madurai
* Chairman: Agastya
* No surviving literary works

### Second Sangam

* Location: Kapadapuram
* Few works survived (not fully available)

### Third Sangam

* Location: Madurai
* Most important Sangam
* Many Tamil literary works available today

---

## 3. GEOGRAPHY OF SANGAM TAMILAKAM

Ancient Tamilakam was divided into five landscapes called “Tinai”:

* Kurinji – Mountains
* Mullai – Forests
* Marutham – Agricultural lands
* Neythal – Coastal areas
* Palai – Desert regions

Each landscape had its own lifestyle, occupation, and culture.

---

## 4. SANGAM KINGDOMS

### 4.1 CHERAS

* Capital: Vanji
* Emblem: Bow and Arrow
* Known for spice trade and elephants
* Port: Muziris

### 4.2 CHOLAS

* Capital: Uraiyur
* Emblem: Tiger
* Known for naval strength and agriculture

### 4.3 PANDYAS

* Capital: Madurai
* Emblem: Fish
* Patronized Sangam literature
* Famous for pearl trade

---

## 5. IMPORTANT SANGAM RULERS

### Chera Kings

* Senguttuvan (Red Chera) – associated with Kannagi story

### Chola Kings

* Karikala Chola
* Built Kallanai (Grand Anicut dam)

### Pandya Kings

* Nedunchezhiyan
* Patron of Tamil Sangam

---

## 6. SANGAM LITERATURE

Sangam literature is the oldest Tamil literature written during Sangam Age.

### Two Major Groups:

* Ettuthogai (Eight Anthologies)
* Pathupattu (Ten Idylls)

---

## 7. ETTUTHOGAI (8 BOOKS)

1. Aingurunuru
2. Narrinai
3. Kurunthogai
4. Akananuru
5. Purananuru
6. Kalithogai
7. Paripadal
8. Pathitrupathu

---

## 8. PATHUPATTU (10 IDYLLS)

1. Thirumurugatrupadai
2. Porunaratruppadai
3. Sirupanarruppadai
4. Perumpanarruppadai
5. Mullaippattu
6. Maduraikkanchi
7. Nedunalvadai
8. Kurinjippattu
9. Pattinappalai
10. Malaipadukadam

---

## 9. IMPORTANT SANGAM POETS

* Kapilar
* Avvaiyar
* Thiruvalluvar (linked period debate but highly important)
* Nakkirar
* Paranar
* Mamulanar

---

## 10. TIRUKKURAL

* Author: Thiruvalluvar
* Contains 1330 couplets
* Deals with ethics, politics, and love
* Considered universal moral guide

---

## 11. SANGAM SOCIETY

### Social Structure:

* No rigid caste system initially
* Occupation-based society
* Women had respected position

### Women in Sangam Age:

* Avvaiyar was a famous poetess
* Women participated in trade and literature

---

## 12. ECONOMY OF SANGAM AGE

### Main Economic Activities:

* Agriculture
* Fishing
* Trade
* Handicrafts

### Trade:

* Internal and foreign trade flourished
* Romans traded with Tamilakam
* Spices, pearls, textiles exported

---

## 13. PORT CITIES

* Puhar (Kaveripattinam) – Chola port
* Muziris – Chera port
* Korkai – Pandya pearl port

---

## 14. FOREIGN CONTACTS

Tamilakam had trade relations with:

* Romans
* Greeks
* Arabs

Roman coins found in Tamil Nadu prove strong trade relations.

---

## 15. RELIGION IN SANGAM AGE

* Early worship of nature
* Murugan worship very important
* Jainism and Buddhism also present
* No dominant single religion

---

## 16. ART AND CULTURE

* Music and dance were highly developed
* Instruments used: Yazh, Parai
* Temple culture in early stage
* Tamil language flourished

---

## 17. WARFARE IN SANGAM AGE

* Kings maintained strong armies
* Infantry, cavalry, elephants used
* Hero stones (Nadukal) were erected for warriors

---

## 18. HERO STONES (NADUKAL)

* Stones raised in memory of soldiers
* Indicate bravery and sacrifice
* Important archaeological evidence

---

## 19. IMPORTANT BATTLES

* Battle between Cheras and Cholas for dominance
* Frequent wars between three Tamil kingdoms

---

## 20. IMPORTANT TERMS

* Tinai – ecological division
* Sangam – assembly of poets
* Nadukal – hero stone
* Yavana – foreigners (Greeks/Romans)
* Purananuru – external life literature
* Akananuru – internal life literature

---

## 21. IMPORTANT FACTS (EXAM POINTS)

✔ Sangam Age = 300 BCE – 300 CE
✔ Three kingdoms = Chera, Chola, Pandya
✔ Ettuthogai = 8 anthologies
✔ Pathupattu = 10 idylls
✔ Kallanai built by Karikala Chola
✔ Muziris = important Chera port
✔ Puhar = Chola port

## 23. SUMMARY

Sangam Age is the foundation of Tamil culture and literature. It shows advanced civilization with rich literature, strong economy, and international trade. It is one of the most important topics for TNPSC exams.
