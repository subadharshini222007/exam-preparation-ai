

# 🏰 MEDIEVAL AGE – INDIAN HISTORY 

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION TO MEDIEVAL INDIA

Medieval Indian history generally refers to the period between the 8th century CE and the 18th century CE. This period marks major political, social, economic and cultural changes in India. It saw the rise of powerful kingdoms in North India and South India, the establishment of the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughal Empire, and regional kingdoms like Vijayanagar and Bahmani kingdoms.

This period is important for TNPSC exams because it includes many repeated topics such as Delhi Sultanate administration, Mughal rulers, Vijayanagar empire achievements, and Bhakti movement.

---

## 2. EARLY MEDIEVAL PERIOD IN INDIA

After the decline of Harsha’s empire, India was divided into many small kingdoms. Important regional powers emerged such as:

* Rajputs in North India
* Palas in Bengal
* Pratiharas in North India
* Rashtrakutas in Deccan
* Cholas, Pandyas, Pallavas in South India

This period is also known as the “Tripartite struggle” between Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas for control over Kannauj.

### Important Features:

* Feudal system development
* Rise of regional kingdoms
* Growth of temple culture
* Development of regional languages

---

## 3. ADVENT OF ISLAM IN INDIA

Islam entered India through:

* Arab conquest of Sindh (Muhammad bin Qasim, 712 CE)
* Turkish invasions (Mahmud of Ghazni, Muhammad of Ghor)

### Mahmud of Ghazni:

* Conducted 17 raids into India
* Attacked Somnath temple
* Main aim: wealth collection

### Muhammad of Ghor:

* Defeated Prithviraj Chauhan in Second Battle of Tarain (1192 CE)
* Established foundation for Delhi Sultanate

---

## 4. DELHI SULTANATE (1206–1526 CE)

The Delhi Sultanate was established after Qutb-ud-din Aibak became ruler in 1206 CE.

### Dynasties:

1. Slave Dynasty
2. Khilji Dynasty
3. Tughlaq Dynasty
4. Sayyid Dynasty
5. Lodi Dynasty

---

### 4.1 SLAVE DYNASTY

#### Qutb-ud-din Aibak

* Founder of Slave Dynasty
* Built Qutb Minar (started)

#### Iltutmish

* Completed Qutb Minar
* Introduced Silver Tanka & Copper Jital
* Formed Chalisa group

#### Razia Sultana

* First female ruler of Delhi Sultanate

#### Balban

* Strong central administration
* Theory of divine kingship (Zil-i-Ilahi)

---

### 4.2 KHILJI DYNASTY

#### Alauddin Khilji

* Market control system
* Price regulation
* Military reforms
* Defeated Mongols
* Malik Kafur led southern expeditions

---

### 4.3 TUGHLAQ DYNASTY

#### Muhammad-bin-Tughlaq

* Token currency experiment failed
* Shifted capital to Daulatabad
* Administrative experiments

#### Firoz Shah Tughlaq

* Built canals
* Promoted irrigation
* Reduced taxes

---

## 5. VIJAYANAGAR EMPIRE (1336–1646 CE)

Founded by Harihara and Bukka in 1336 CE.

### Important rulers:

* Krishnadevaraya (greatest ruler)

### Achievements:

* Strong administration
* Trade development
* Cultural growth
* Construction of temples

### Krishnadevaraya:

* Author of “Amuktamalyada”
* Won wars against Bahmani Sultanates
* Promoted literature and art

### Important Features:

* Hampi capital
* Foreign travelers like Domingo Paes described empire wealth
* Strong army system

---

## 6. BAHMANI KINGDOM

* Established in 1347 CE
* Founder: Ala-ud-din Hasan Bahman Shah

### Features:

* Divided Deccan into provinces
* Strong administration
* Cultural mixing of Hindu and Islamic traditions

---

## 7. MUGHAL EMPIRE (1526–1857 CE)

Founded by Babur after First Battle of Panipat (1526 CE).

---

### 7.1 BABUR

* Founder of Mughal Empire
* Defeated Ibrahim Lodi

---

### 7.2 HUMAYUN

* Lost empire initially
* Regained throne with Persian help

---

### 7.3 SHER SHAH SURI

* Built Grand Trunk Road
* Introduced currency system
* Strong administration

---

### 7.4 AKBAR (GREATEST MUGHAL RULER)

* Religious tolerance policy
* Abolished Jizya tax
* Din-i-Ilahi religion
* Strong central administration

---

### 7.5 JAHANGIR

* Justice system improved
* Nur Jahan influence

---

### 7.6 SHAH JAHAN

* Golden age of architecture
* Built Taj Mahal

---

### 7.7 AURANGZEB

* Expanded empire
* Strict Islamic policies
* Long wars weakened empire

---

## 8. SOUTH INDIA MEDIEVAL KINGDOMS

### Cholas

* Strong naval power
* Brihadeeswarar temple built by Rajaraja I
* Efficient local administration (Uthiramerur inscription)

### Pandyas

* Controlled southern Tamil Nadu
* Maritime trade

### Pallavas

* Rock-cut temples at Mahabalipuram
* Art and architecture development

---

## 9. BHAKTI MOVEMENT

Bhakti movement emphasized devotion to God rather than rituals.

### Saints:

* Ramanuja
* Kabir
* Guru Nanak
* Chaitanya

### Features:

* Unity among people
* Opposed caste system
* Promoted equality

---

## 10. SUFI MOVEMENT

* Islamic mystic movement
* Promoted peace and love
* Khanqah centers

---

## 11. IMPORTANT TERMS (TNPSC REVISION)

* Iqta system – land revenue assignment
* Jizya – tax on non-Muslims
* Mansabdari system – Mughal military ranking
* Zabt system – land revenue system
* Zamindars – land holders

---

## 12. IMPORTANT BATTLES

* First Battle of Tarain (1191) – Prithviraj vs Muhammad of Ghor
* Second Battle of Tarain (1192) – establishment of Delhi Sultanate
* First Battle of Panipat (1526) – Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi
* Battle of Talikota (1565) – downfall of Vijayanagar
