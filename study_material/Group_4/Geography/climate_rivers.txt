
# 🌦️🌊 INDIAN CLIMATE & RIVERS

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION

India has a highly diverse climate and river system due to its vast geographical extent, varying altitude, and monsoon influence. Climate and rivers are interlinked because rainfall patterns directly affect river flow, agriculture, and water resources. India’s climate is primarily governed by the monsoon system, while its rivers are divided into Himalayan and Peninsular systems.

---

# 🌦️ PART 1: INDIAN CLIMATE

---

# 🟡 1. FACTORS AFFECTING INDIAN CLIMATE

India’s climate is influenced by multiple geographic and atmospheric factors:

### 🔵 1. LATITUDE

India lies between 8°N and 37°N, placing it in tropical and subtropical zones.

### 🔵 2. ALTITUDE

Temperature decreases with altitude. Himalayas have cold climate, plains have moderate climate.

### 🔵 3. DISTANCE FROM SEA

Coastal areas have moderate climate, interior regions have extreme climate.

### 🔵 4. HIMALAYAS

Act as a barrier against cold winds from Central Asia.

### 🔵 5. MONSOON WINDS

Seasonal reversal of winds controls rainfall pattern.

---

# 🌧️ 2. MONSOON SYSTEM OF INDIA

Monsoon is the most important climatic feature of India.

### 🟢 DEFINITION:

Monsoon refers to seasonal reversal of wind direction causing rainfall in summer.

---

## 🌊 SOUTHWEST MONSOON (JUNE–SEPTEMBER)

### 🔵 FEATURES:

* Brings 75% of rainfall
* Originates from Arabian Sea & Bay of Bengal
* Divided into two branches:

  * Arabian Sea Branch
  * Bay of Bengal Branch

### 🔵 IMPORTANCE:

* Agriculture depends on it
* River flow increases
* Reservoirs filled

---

## 🌧️ NORTHEAST MONSOON (OCT–DEC)

### 🔵 FEATURES:

* Affects Tamil Nadu heavily
* Winds blow from land to sea
* Brings cyclonic rainfall

---

# 🌡️ 3. SEASONS OF INDIA

### 🟡 4 MAIN SEASONS:

### 🔵 1. SUMMER (MARCH–JUNE)

* High temperature
* Heat waves in northern India

### 🔵 2. SOUTHWEST MONSOON (JUNE–SEPTEMBER)

* Heavy rainfall
* Flooding in rivers

### 🔵 3. RETREATING MONSOON (OCT–NOV)

* Cyclones in Bay of Bengal

### 🔵 4. WINTER (DEC–FEB)

* Cold climate in north India
* Snowfall in Himalayas

---

# 🌦️ 4. RAINFALL DISTRIBUTION IN INDIA

### 🟢 HIGHEST RAINFALL:

* Meghalaya (Mawsynram, Cherrapunji)
* Western Ghats

### 🔴 LOW RAINFALL:

* Rajasthan (Thar Desert)
* Ladakh region

---

# 🟣 1. CLIMATE REGIONS OF INDIA

India can be divided into different climate regions based on rainfall and temperature:

TYPES:
Tropical Monsoon Climate (most of India)
Arid Climate (Rajasthan)
Semi-arid Climate (Deccan Plateau edges)
Mountain Climate (Himalayas)
Coastal Climate (Kerala, TN, Maharashtra coast)



# 🌬️ 2. JET STREAMS AND INDIA

Jet streams are fast flowing winds in the upper atmosphere.

### 🔵 IMPORTANCE:

* Control monsoon arrival
* Influence rainfall strength
* Affect winter storms in North India

---

# 🌧️ 3. EL NIÑO & LA NIÑA EFFECT

### 🔴 EL NIÑO:

* Ocean warming in Pacific
* Weak monsoon in India
* Drought risk increases

### 🔵 LA NIÑA:

* Ocean cooling
* Strong monsoon
* Heavy rainfall & floods

---

# 🌡️ 4. TEMPERATURE DISTRIBUTION IN INDIA

### 🔵 EXTREMES:

* Highest temperature → Rajasthan (Phalodi)
* Lowest temperature → Drass (Ladakh)

### 🟡 REASON:

* Latitude difference
* Altitude variation
* Distance from sea

---

# 🌪️ 5. CYCLONES IN INDIA

### 🔵 AREAS AFFECTED:

* Bay of Bengal coast (high risk)
* Odisha, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh
* Arabian Sea (less frequent but strong)

### 🔴 EFFECTS:

* Heavy rainfall
* Floods
* Wind destruction

---

# 🌧️ 6. MONSOON BURST

A sudden very heavy rainfall during monsoon is called monsoon burst.

### 🔵 FEATURES:

* Short duration
* Very high intensity
* Causes flash floods

---

# 🌦️ 7. OROGRAPHIC RAINFALL

### 🔵 MEANING:

Rainfall caused when moist air rises over mountains.

### 🟢 EXAMPLE:

* Western Ghats → heavy rainfall on west side
* Deccan Plateau → rain shadow region

---

# 🌍 8. CLIMATE CHANGE IN INDIA

### 🔴 EFFECTS:

* Rising temperatures
* Glacier melting in Himalayas
* Sea level rise in coastal areas
* Irregular monsoon

---

# 🌡️ 9. GLOBAL WARMING EFFECTS ON INDIA

### 🔵 IMPACTS:

* Heat waves increase
* Agriculture productivity reduces
* Water scarcity increases
* Cyclones become stronger

---

# 🌧️ 10. MONSOON ANOMALIES

### 🔴 TYPES:

* Delayed monsoon
* Weak monsoon
* Excess rainfall monsoon

### 🔵 REASONS:

* El Niño
* Jet stream shift
* Ocean temperature changes

---

# 🌿 11. MICRO CLIMATES IN INDIA

### 🔵 TYPES:

* Urban heat island (cities hotter)
* Forest microclimate (cool & humid)
* Hill station climate (cold)
* Coastal climate (moderate)

---

# 🌬️ 12. WINDS IN INDIA

### 🔵 IMPORTANT WINDS:

* Southwest monsoon winds
* Northeast monsoon winds
* Local winds (loo – hot wind in north India)

---

# 🔥 QUICK REVISION POINTS

* India = Monsoon climate country
* El Niño = weak monsoon
* La Niña = strong monsoon
* Western Ghats = orographic rainfall
* Rajasthan = hot desert climate
* Himalayas = cold mountain climate
* Cyclones = Bay of Bengal high risk

# 🌊 PART 2: INDIAN RIVER SYSTEM

---

# 🟡 5. CLASSIFICATION OF RIVERS

India has two major river systems:

### 🔵 1. HIMALAYAN RIVERS

* Perennial (flow year-round)
* Snow + rain-fed
* Create fertile plains

### 🔵 2. PENINSULAR RIVERS

* Seasonal
* Rain-fed
* Flow through plateau regions

---

# 🏔️ 6. HIMALAYAN RIVER SYSTEM

### 🟢 FEATURES:

* Originate from Himalayas
* Perennial rivers
* Large basins
* Form floodplains

---

## 🌊 IMPORTANT HIMALAYAN RIVERS

### 🔵 1. GANGA RIVER SYSTEM

* Origin: Gangotri Glacier
* Tributaries: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak
* Forms fertile Indo-Gangetic plain

---

### 🔵 2. BRAHMAPUTRA RIVER

* Origin: Tibet (Tsangpo)
* Enters India through Arunachal Pradesh
* Causes frequent floods in Assam

---

### 🔵 3. INDUS RIVER SYSTEM

* Origin: Tibet
* Flows through Ladakh
* Major tributaries in India

---

# 🌾 7. PENINSULAR RIVER SYSTEM

### 🟡 FEATURES:

* Originate from Western Ghats
* Flow through plateau
* Seasonal rivers
* Narrow valleys

---

## 🌊 IMPORTANT PENINSULAR RIVERS

### 🔵 GODAVARI

* Longest peninsular river
* Called “Dakshin Ganga”

### 🔵 KRISHNA

* Flows through Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra

### 🔵 CAUVERY

* Important for Tamil Nadu agriculture

### 🔵 NARMADA

* West flowing river
* Forms rift valley

---

# 🌊 8. RIVER BASINS OF INDIA

River basins are areas drained by a river and its tributaries.

### 🔵 IMPORTANT BASINS:

* Ganga Basin (largest)
* Brahmaputra Basin
* Godavari Basin
* Krishna Basin
* Cauvery Basin

---

# 🌧️ 9. RIVER EROSION & LANDFORMS

### 🟢 EROSIONAL LANDFORMS:

* V-shaped valley
* Waterfalls
* Gorges

### 🟣 DEPOSITIONAL LANDFORMS:

* Flood plains
* Deltas
* Oxbow lakes

---

# 🌊 10. DELTAS OF INDIA

### 🟡 IMPORTANT DELTAS:

* Ganga-Brahmaputra Delta (largest in world)
* Godavari Delta
* Krishna Delta
* Cauvery Delta

---

# ⚠️ 11. FLOODS AND DROUGHTS IN INDIA

### 🔵 FLOODS:

* Caused by heavy rainfall
* Common in Assam, Bihar, West Bengal

### 🔴 DROUGHTS:

* Caused by monsoon failure
* Common in Rajasthan, Maharashtra

---

# 🌍 12. IMPORTANCE OF CLIMATE & RIVERS

* Agriculture depends on monsoon
* Rivers support irrigation
* Hydropower generation
* Transport and trade
* Ecosystem balance

---

* Monsoon = seasonal wind system
* SW monsoon = main rainfall source
* Himalayan rivers = perennial
* Peninsular rivers = seasonal
* Ganga basin = largest in India
* Floods = NE India
