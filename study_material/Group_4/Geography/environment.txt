
PART A - NATURAL DISASTERS

1. INTRODUCTION

Natural disasters are sudden events caused by natural processes that result in loss of
life, property and environment.

Examples:
- Earthquakes
- Floods
- Cyclones
- Droughts
- Landslides
- Volcanic eruptions
- Tsunamis

2. EARTHQUAKES

2.1 Causes

- Movement of tectonic plates
- Faulting
- Volcanic activity

2.2 Effects

- Loss of life and property
- Damage to buildings and infrastructure
- Fires
- Landslides
- Tsunamis in coastal areas

2.3 Earthquake-Prone Areas

- Himalayan region
- North-East India
- Gujarat
- Parts of Maharashtra and Uttarakhand

2.4 Safety Measures

- Earthquake-resistant buildings
- Early warning systems
- Public awareness
- Emergency drills

3. FLOODS

3.1 Causes

- Heavy rainfall
- Cyclones
- River overflow
- Dam failure
- Poor drainage

3.2 Effects

- Loss of life and property
- Crop damage
- Spread of diseases
- Displacement of people

3.3 Flood-Prone Areas

- Assam
- Bihar
- West Bengal
- Uttar Pradesh
- Kerala

3.4 Safety Measures

- Flood forecasting
- Embankments
- Dams and reservoirs
- Afforestation
- Public awareness

4. CYCLONES

4.1 Causes

- Low pressure area over warm sea
- Evaporation of sea water
- Coriolis force

4.2 Effects

- Heavy rainfall
- Strong winds
- Storm surge
- Flooding
- Damage to coastal areas

4.3 Cyclone-Prone Areas

- Bay of Bengal coast
- Tamil Nadu
- Andhra Pradesh
- Odisha
- West Bengal

4.4 Safety Measures

- Cyclone shelters
- Early warning systems
- Evacuation plans
- Mangrove protection

5. DROUGHTS

5.1 Causes

- Low rainfall
- Failure of monsoon
- Deforestation
- Overuse of groundwater

5.2 Effects

- Crop failure
- Water scarcity
- Migration
- Food shortage

5.3 Drought-Prone Areas

- Rajasthan
- Gujarat
- Parts of Maharashtra
- Parts of Karnataka

5.4 Safety Measures

- Watershed management
- Rainwater harvesting
- Drip irrigation
- Drought-resistant crops

6. LANDSLIDES

6.1 Causes

- Heavy rainfall
- Deforestation
- Earthquakes
- Construction on slopes

6.2 Landslide-Prone Areas

- Himalayas
- Western Ghats
- Nilgiris
- North-East India

6.3 Safety Measures

- Afforestation
- Slope stabilisation
- Controlled construction

7. TSUNAMIS

7.1 Causes

- Undersea earthquakes
- Volcanic eruptions

7.2 Effects

- Coastal flooding
- Loss of life and property
- Damage to mangroves

7.3 Safety Measures

- Early warning systems
- Evacuation
- Mangrove protection

PART B - ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

1. INTRODUCTION

Environmental protection refers to measures taken to conserve natural resources and
prevent pollution.

2. TYPES OF ENVIRONMENTAL POLLUTION

1. Air pollution
2. Water pollution
3. Soil pollution
4. Noise pollution

3. AIR POLLUTION

3.1 Causes

- Industrial emissions
- Vehicle exhaust
- Burning of fossil fuels
- Construction dust

3.2 Effects

- Respiratory diseases
- Acid rain
- Global warming

3.3 Control Measures

- Clean fuels
- Electric vehicles
- Public transport
- Afforestation

4. WATER POLLUTION

4.1 Causes

- Industrial waste
- Sewage
- Agricultural runoff
- Oil spills

4.2 Control Measures

- Treatment of industrial waste
- Sewage treatment
- Rainwater harvesting
- Watershed management

5. SOIL POLLUTION

5.1 Causes

- Excess fertilisers
- Pesticides
- Industrial waste
- Solid waste

5.2 Control Measures

- Organic farming
- Crop rotation
- Proper waste disposal
- Bioremediation

6. NOISE POLLUTION

6.1 Causes

- Traffic
- Industries
- Construction
- Loudspeakers

6.2 Control Measures

- Noise barriers
- Restrictions on loudspeakers
- Green belts

7. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION MEASURES

- Afforestation
- Reforestation
- Wildlife conservation
- National parks and sanctuaries
- Biosphere reserves
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)
- Pollution control laws

8. IMPORTANT ENVIRONMENTAL LAWS AND PROGRAMMES

1. Environment Protection Act - 1986
2. National Green Tribunal - 2010
3. National Action Plan on Climate Change - 2008
4. Paris Agreement - 2015
5. National Afforestation Programme
6. Clean India Mission

9. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Natural disasters cause loss of life and property
- Earthquakes are caused by tectonic movements
- Floods are caused by heavy rainfall and river overflow
- Cyclones affect coastal areas
- Droughts are caused by low rainfall
- Landslides occur in hilly areas
- Tsunamis are caused by undersea earthquakes
- Environmental protection conserves resources
- Pollution can be controlled by clean technology and laws

10. POSSIBLE EXAM QUESTIONS

1. What are natural disasters?
Answer: Sudden events caused by natural processes.

2. What causes earthquakes?
Answer: Movement of tectonic plates.

3. What causes floods?
Answer: Heavy rainfall and river overflow.

4. Which coast is more affected by cyclones?
Answer: Eastern coast.

5. What causes droughts?
Answer: Low rainfall and failure of monsoon.

6. What causes landslides?
Answer: Heavy rainfall and deforestation.

7. What causes tsunamis?
Answer: Undersea earthquakes.

8. What is air pollution?
Answer: Contamination of air by harmful substances.

9. What is water pollution?
Answer: Contamination of water bodies.

10. What is environmental protection?
Answer: Measures to conserve natural resources and prevent pollution.

11. Which law protects the environment in India?
Answer: Environment Protection Act, 1986.

12. Which body handles environmental disputes in India?
Answer: National Green Tribunal.

13. Which programme aims at climate change mitigation?
Answer: National Action Plan on Climate Change.

14. Mention one control measure for air pollution.
Answer: Use of clean fuels or electric vehicles.

15. Mention one control measure for water pollution.
Answer: Treatment of industrial waste.
