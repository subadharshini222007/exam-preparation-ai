# 🌾 AGRICULTURE IN INDIA

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION

Agriculture is the **backbone of the Indian economy**, providing livelihood to a large section of the population. It contributes significantly to **GDP, employment, and food security**.

---

# 🟡 FEATURES OF INDIAN AGRICULTURE

## 🔵 Subsistence + Commercial farming

## 🔵 Dependence on monsoon

## 🔵 Small & fragmented land holdings

## 🔵 Labour-intensive

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 Low productivity compared to developed countries
👉 High population pressure on land

---

## 🔵 Commercial Farming
