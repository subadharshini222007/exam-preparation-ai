# 📊 ECONOMIC PLANNING IN INDIA

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION TO ECONOMIC PLANNING

Economic planning refers to the **systematic allocation of resources** by the government to achieve economic and social objectives such as growth, equity, and stability.

---

## 🔵 NEED FOR PLANNING IN INDIA

* Poverty reduction
* Employment generation
* Balanced regional development
* Industrialization
* Self-reliance

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

India adopted planning because:

* Colonial economy was weak
* Resources were unevenly distributed
* Need for state intervention

---

# 🟡 TYPES OF ECONOMIC PLANNING

## 🔵 CENTRALIZED PLANNING

* Government controls all decisions
* Example: Socialist model

## 🔵 DECENTRALIZED PLANNING

* Local bodies participate

## 🔵 MIXED PLANNING (INDIA)

* Public + Private sector

---

# 🔵 1. PLANNING vs MARKET ECONOMY

## 🟢 PLANNED ECONOMY

* Government decides:

  * What to produce
  * How to produce
  * For whom to produce

### ✔ Advantages

* Reduces inequality
* Focus on welfare
* Balanced development

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Inefficiency
* Bureaucratic delays
* Lack of innovation

---

## 🟡 MARKET ECONOMY

* Decisions based on **demand & supply**

### ✔ Advantages

* Efficiency
* Innovation
* Consumer choice

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Income inequality
* Monopoly
* Exploitation

---

## 🔴 INDIAN MODEL (IMPORTANT)

👉 India follows **Mixed Economy**

## 🧠 ANALYSIS (WRITE IN EXAM)

* Planning ensures equity
* Market ensures efficiency
  👉 **Combination is best for India**

---

# 🔵 2. CENTRALIZATION vs DECENTRALIZATION

## 🟢 CENTRALIZED PLANNING

* Decisions taken by central government

### ✔ Advantages

* Uniform policy
* Strong control

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Ignores local needs
* Slow implementation

---

## 🟡 DECENTRALIZED PLANNING

* Local bodies participate

### ✔ Advantages

* Region-specific development
* Faster execution

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Lack of coordination
* Resource limitations

---

## 🔴 INDIAN APPROACH

👉 Shift from centralized → decentralized (NITI Aayog)

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

* Centralization = control
* Decentralization = participation
  👉 **Balanced approach needed**

---
