# 🏦 1. RESERVE BANK OF INDIA (RBI)

## 🔵 ESTABLISHMENT

* 1935

---

## 🔵 FUNCTIONS

### 🟢 Issue of Currency

* Sole authority to print currency

### 🟢 Banker to Government

* Maintains govt accounts

### 🟢 Banker’s Bank

* Lends to commercial banks

### 🟢 Controller of Credit

* Uses monetary policy

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 RBI = “Monetary authority + financial stability”

# 💱 MONEY & INFLATION

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION TO MONEY

Money is anything that is **generally accepted as a medium of exchange** for goods and services. It plays a crucial role in modern economies.

---

# 🟡 EVOLUTION OF MONEY

## 🔵 BARTER SYSTEM

* Exchange of goods without money

### ❌ Problems

* Double coincidence of wants
* Lack of common measure

---

## 🔵 COMMODITY MONEY

* Gold, silver

---

## 🔵 PAPER MONEY

* Issued by government

---

## 🔵 DIGITAL MONEY (MODERN)

* UPI, cards

---

# 🔵 FUNCTIONS OF MONEY

---

## 🟢 PRIMARY FUNCTIONS

* Medium of exchange
* Unit of account

---

## 🟡 SECONDARY FUNCTIONS

* Store of value
* Standard of deferred payment

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 Money simplifies trade and boosts economic growth

---

# 🟣 TYPES OF MONEY

---

## 🔵 FIAT MONEY

* Legal tender issued by govt

## 🔵 FIDUCIARY MONEY

* Based on trust (cheques)

## 🔵 NEAR MONEY

* Bonds, treasury bills

---

# 🏦 MONEY SUPPLY

---

## 🔵 MEASURES

* M1 (narrow money)
* M3 (broad money)

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 More money supply → more demand → inflation

---

# 💸 INFLATION (VERY IMPORTANT 🔥)

---

## 🔵 DEFINITION

Inflation is a **continuous rise in general price level**

---

# 🟡 TYPES OF INFLATION

---

## 🔵 DEMAND-PULL

* Demand > Supply

---

## 🔵 COST-PUSH

* Increase in cost of production

---

## 🔵 STRUCTURAL INFLATION

* Supply bottlenecks

---

## 🔵 HYPERINFLATION

* Extremely high inflation

---

# 📊 MEASUREMENT OF INFLATION

---

## 🔵 CPI (Consumer Price Index)

* Measures retail prices

---

## 🔵 WPI (Wholesale Price Index)

* Measures wholesale prices

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 CPI reflects real cost of living

---

# ⚖️ EFFECTS OF INFLATION

---

## 🔴 NEGATIVE EFFECTS

* Reduces purchasing power
* Affects poor people
* Uncertainty in economy

---

## 🟢 POSITIVE EFFECTS

* Encourages investment
* Reduces real debt burden

---

# 🏦 CONTROL OF INFLATION

---

## 🔵 MONETARY POLICY (RBI)

* Increase repo rate
* Reduce money supply

---

## 🔵 FISCAL POLICY

* Reduce govt spending
* Increase taxes

---

## 🔵 SUPPLY-SIDE MEASURES

* Increase production
* Improve distribution

---

# 🔄 DEFLATION & DISINFLATION

---

## 🔵 DEFLATION

* Fall in price level

## 🔵 DISINFLATION

* Slowdown in inflation rate

---

# 📉 PHILLIPS CURVE (ADVANCED 🔥)

---

## 🔵 CONCEPT

* Trade-off between inflation & unemployment

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 Low unemployment → high inflation

---

# 🌍 INFLATION IN INDIA

## 🔵 CONTROL MEASURES
