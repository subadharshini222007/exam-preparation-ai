# 🟢 INTRODUCTION TO ECONOMICS

Economics is the study of how individuals and societies use limited resources to satisfy unlimited wants. It deals with production, distribution, exchange, and consumption of goods and services.

---

# 🟡 BASIC ECONOMIC CONCEPTS

* Resources are limited, wants are unlimited

### 🔵 CHOICE

* Selection of best alternative

### 🔵 OPPORTUNITY COST

* Cost of next best alternative

### 🔵 CONSUMPTION

# 🧾 1. GOVERNMENT BUDGET 

## 🔵 DEFINITION

Government budget is an **annual financial statement** of income and expenditure of the government.

---

## 🟡 COMPONENTS OF BUDGET

### 🔵 1. REVENUE RECEIPTS

* Tax revenue
* Non-tax revenue (fees, fines, dividends)

### 🔵 2. CAPITAL RECEIPTS

* Borrowings
* Disinvestment

---

### 🔵 3. REVENUE EXPENDITURE

* Salaries
* Subsidies
* Interest payments

### 🔵 4. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE

* Infrastructure
* Asset creation

---

## 🔴 TYPES OF DEFICITS

### 🔵 Fiscal Deficit

= Total expenditure – Total receipts (excluding borrowings)

### 🔵 Revenue Deficit

= Revenue expenditure – Revenue receipts

### 🔵 Primary Deficit

= Fiscal deficit – Interest payments

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS (VERY IMPORTANT)

* High fiscal deficit → inflation risk
* Capital expenditure → long-term growth
* Revenue expenditure → short-term consumption

---

# 💸 2. TAXATION SYSTEM IN INDIA

## 🔵 DEFINITION

Tax is a **compulsory payment** to government without direct benefit.

---

## 🟡 TYPES OF TAXES

### 🔵 DIRECT TAX

* Paid directly to government
* Example: Income Tax

✔ Progressive (rich pay more)

---

### 🔵 INDIRECT TAX

* Collected via goods/services
* Example: GST

✔ Regressive (affects all equally)

---

## 🔥 GST (VERY IMPORTANT)

### 🔵 FEATURES

* One Nation, One Tax
* Replaced multiple taxes

### 🔵 TYPES

* CGST (Central)
* SGST (State)
* IGST (Interstate)

---

## 🔴 PROBLEMS IN TAX SYSTEM

* Tax evasion
* Complex compliance
* Narrow tax base

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

* Direct tax = equality
* Indirect tax = easy collection

---

# 🏦 3. MONETARY POLICY (ADVANCED)

## 🔵 DEFINITION

Policy used by RBI to control **money supply & inflation**

---

## 🟡 TOOLS

### 🔵 Repo Rate

* Rate at which RBI lends to banks

### 🔵 Reverse Repo Rate

* RBI borrows from banks

### 🔵 CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio)

* Cash banks keep with RBI

### 🔵 SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio)

* Liquid assets maintained by banks

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

* Increase rates → reduce inflation
* Decrease rates → increase growth
