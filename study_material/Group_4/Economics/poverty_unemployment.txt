# 📉 POVERTY IN INDIA

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION

Poverty refers to a condition in which a person is **unable to meet basic needs** such as food, clothing, shelter, education, and healthcare. It is not only economic deprivation but also **social exclusion and lack of opportunities**.

---

# 🟡 TYPES OF POVERTY

## 🔵 1. ABSOLUTE POVERTY

* Lack of basic necessities
* Measured by poverty line

## 🔵 2. RELATIVE POVERTY

* Comparison with others in society
* Reflects inequality

## 🔵 3. RURAL POVERTY

* Agriculture-based
* Seasonal income

## 🔵 4. URBAN POVERTY

* Slums
* Unemployment

---

# 🟣 POVERTY LINE IN INDIA

## 🔵 DEFINITION

---

# 📉 UNEMPLOYMENT IN INDIA

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION

Unemployment refers to a situation where a person who is **willing and able to work** is unable to find a job at the prevailing wage rate. It is a major socio-economic problem affecting growth and development.

---

# 🟡 TYPES OF UNEMPLOYMENT

---

## 🔵 1. SEASONAL UNEMPLOYMENT

* Found in agriculture
* Work available only during certain seasons

👉 Example: Farmers idle after harvest

---

## 🔵 2. DISGUISED UNEMPLOYMENT (VERY IMPORTANT 🔥)

* More people employed than required
* Marginal productivity = zero

👉 Example: 5 பேர் வேலை, but 3 பேர் போதுமே

---

## 🔵 3. STRUCTURAL UNEMPLOYMENT

* Mismatch between skills & jobs

👉 Example: Engineering graduate but no suitable job

---

## 🔵 4. FRICTIONAL UNEMPLOYMENT

* Temporary unemployment while changing jobs

---

## 🔵 5. CYCLICAL UNEMPLOYMENT

* Due to economic slowdown

---

## 🔵 6. EDUCATED UNEMPLOYMENT (IMPORTANT)

* Degree holders without jobs

---

# 🔴 CAUSES OF UNEMPLOYMENT

---

## 🔵 ECONOMIC CAUSES

* Slow industrial growth
* Low investment
* Lack of infrastructure

---

## 🔵 SOCIAL CAUSES

* Education system not skill-based
* Preference for white-collar jobs

---

## 🔵 DEMOGRAPHIC CAUSES

* Rapid population growth

---

## 🔵 TECHNOLOGICAL CAUSES

* Automation reduces jobs

---

# 📊 MEASUREMENT OF UNEMPLOYMENT

---

## 🔵 UNEMPLOYMENT RATE

* % of labour force without jobs

---

## 🔵 LABOUR FORCE PARTICIPATION RATE (LFPR)

* % of working-age population working or seeking work

---

## 🔵 WORKER POPULATION RATIO (WPR)

* % of population actually employed

---

## 🧠 ANALYSIS

👉 Low LFPR = hidden unemployment

---

# 🌾 RURAL UNEMPLOYMENT

---

## 🔵 FEATURES

* Seasonal + disguised unemployment

---

## 🔴 PROBLEMS

* Small land holdings
* Lack of irrigation
* Low productivity

---

# 🏙️ URBAN UNEMPLOYMENT

---

## 🔵 FEATURES

* Educated unemployment
* Informal sector jobs

---

## 🔴 PROBLEMS

* Job competition
* Skill mismatch

---

# ⚖️ UNDEREMPLOYMENT

* People working below their capacity
  👉 Example: Engineer working as clerk

---

# 🔄 RELATION WITH POVERTY
