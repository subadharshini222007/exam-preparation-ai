# 🟢 1. BASIC PERCENTAGE (X% of Y)

## 📌 How to Solve (Steps)

1. Percentage-ஐ fraction-ஆ மாற்று (X/100)
2. Multiply with number (Y)
3. Simplify

---

## ✍️ Solved Examples

### 🧠 Example 1: 20% of 200

👉 Step 1: 20% = 20/100
👉 Step 2: (20/100) × 200
👉 Step 3: 20 × 2 = 40

✔ Answer = 40

---

### 🧠 Example 2: 15% of 300

👉 (15/100) × 300
👉 15 × 3 = 45

✔ Answer = 45

---

### 🧠 Example 3: 25% of 400

👉 (25/100) × 400
👉 25 × 4 = 100

✔ Answer = 100

---

### 🧠 Example 4: 12% of 500

👉 (12/100) × 500
👉 12 × 5 = 60

✔ Answer = 60

---

### 🧠 Example 5: 5% of 1000

👉 (5/100) × 1000
👉 5 × 10 = 50

✔ Answer = 50

---

# 🟡 2. FIND PERCENTAGE (PART / WHOLE)

## 📌 Steps

1. Part ÷ Whole
2. × 100

---

## ✍️ Examples

### 🧠 Example 1: 50 is what % of 200

👉 50/200 × 100
👉 1/4 × 100 = 25%

✔ Answer = 25%

---

### 🧠 Example 2: 30 is what % of 150

👉 30/150 × 100
👉 1/5 × 100 = 20%

✔ Answer = 20%

---

### 🧠 Example 3: 75 is what % of 300

👉 75/300 × 100
👉 1/4 × 100 = 25%

✔ Answer = 25%

---

# 🔵 3. PERCENTAGE INCREASE / DECREASE

## 📌 Steps

1. New - Old
2. Divide by Old
3. × 100

---

## ✍️ Examples

### 🧠 Example 1: 100 → 120

👉 (120 - 100) / 100 × 100
👉 20% increase

---

### 🧠 Example 2: 200 → 150

👉 (150 - 200) / 200 × 100
👉 -25% → 25% decrease

---

### 🧠 Example 3: 500 → 600

👉 100/500 × 100 = 20%

---

### 🧠 Example 4: 400 → 360

👉 40/400 × 100 = 10% decrease

---

---

# 🟣 4. SUCCESSIVE PERCENTAGE

## 📌 Steps

Use shortcut formula:
👉 A + B + (AB/100)

---

## ✍️ Examples

### 🧠 Example 1: +10%, +20%

👉 10 + 20 + (200/100)
👉 10 + 20 + 2 = 32%

✔ Answer = 32% increase

---

### 🧠 Example 2: +20%, -10%

👉 20 - 10 - 2 = 8%

---

### 🧠 Example 3: -20%, -10%

👉 -20 -10 +2 = -28%

✔ 28% decrease

---

# 🟠 5. PROFIT & LOSS (PERCENTAGE BASED)

## 📌 Steps

1. Find difference (SP - CP)
2. Divide by CP
3. × 100

---

## ✍️ Examples

### 🧠 Example 1

CP = 200, SP = 250
👉 Profit = 50
👉 50/200 × 100 = 25%

✔ Answer = 25% profit

---

### 🧠 Example 2

CP = 400, SP = 360
👉 Loss = 40
👉 40/400 × 100 = 10%

✔ Answer = 10% loss

---

### 🧠 Example 3

MP = 1000, SP = 800
👉 Discount = 200
👉 200/1000 × 100 = 20%

✔ Answer = 20% discount

---
TYPE 5: PROFIT / LOSS / DISCOUNT TYPE

Used in business problems.

📌 Formula:

👉 Profit % = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100
👉 Discount % = (MP - SP) / MP × 100

📌 How exam asks:
Marked price vs selling price
Profit calculation
📌 Example:

MP = 1000, SP = 800
Discount = 20%

📌 Practice:
MP 500, SP 450
CP 200, SP 250

TYPE 6: POPULATION / COMPOUND GROWTH

📌 Formula

📌 Concept

Final value தெரியும், original find பண்ணணும்

📌 Formula
