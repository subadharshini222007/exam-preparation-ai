# 🌍 NATURAL RESOURCES OF INDIA

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION TO RESOURCES

Resources are materials available in the environment that can be used to satisfy human needs. India is rich in natural resources such as land, water, minerals, forests, and energy resources. Proper utilization and conservation of resources are essential for sustainable development.

---

# 🟡 TYPES OF RESOURCES 

### 🔵 BASED ON STATUS OF DEVELOPMENT

* Potential resources
* Developed resources
* Stock
* Reserves

### 🔵 BASED ON DISTRIBUTION

* Ubiquitous (available everywhere → air)
* Localized (minerals)
