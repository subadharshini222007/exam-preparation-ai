# 🌍 TAMIL NADU GEOGRAPHY

---

# 🟢 INTRODUCTION

Tamil Nadu is located in the southern part of India. It has diverse physical features such as mountains, plateaus, plains, rivers, and coastal regions. It plays a major role in agriculture, industry, and culture.

---

# 🟡 1. LOCATION AND EXTENT

### 🔵 LATITUDINAL & LONGITUDINAL EXTENT

* 8°N to 13°N latitude
* 76°E to 80°E longitude

### 🔵 BOUNDARIES

* North → Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka
* West → Kerala
* East → Bay of Bengal
* South → Indian Ocean

---

# 🏔️ 2. PHYSICAL FEATURES OF TAMIL NADU

Tamil Nadu has 3 major physical divisions:

### 🔵 1. WESTERN GHATS

* Nilgiri Hills
* Anaimalai Hills
* Palani Hills

### 🔵 2. EASTERN GHATS

* Discontinuous hills
* Javadi, Shevaroy, Kalrayan hills

### 🔵 3. PLAINS

* Coastal plains
* Cauvery delta (fertile region)

---

# 🌊 3. RIVER SYSTEM OF TAMIL NADU

### 🔵 MAJOR RIVERS

* Cauvery (most important)
* Vaigai
* Palar
* Tamirabarani

---

## 🟢 FEATURES

* Mostly seasonal rivers
* Depend on monsoon rainfall

---

## 🌾 CAUVERY DELTA

* Known as “Rice bowl of Tamil Nadu”
* Highly fertile

---

# 🌦️ 4. CLIMATE OF TAMIL NADU

### 🔵 TYPE

* Tropical climate

---

## 🌧️ MONSOON

* Southwest monsoon → less rainfall
* Northeast monsoon → main rainfall source

---

## 🌡️ TEMPERATURE

* High temperature in summer
* Moderate in coastal areas

---

# 🌧️ 5. RAINFALL DISTRIBUTION

### 🔵 HIGH RAINFALL

* Western Ghats
* Nilgiris

### 🔴 LOW RAINFALL

* Ramanathapuram
* Interior districts

---

# 🌱 6. SOILS OF TAMIL NADU

### 🔵 TYPES

* Red soil
* Black soil
* Alluvial soil
* Laterite soil

---

## 🟢 IMPORTANCE

* Determines crop pattern
* Influences agriculture
