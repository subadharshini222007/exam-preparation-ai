
## Unit 1: Basics of Indian Economy

- India follows a **Mixed Economy** model.
- Major sectors: Primary (Agriculture), Secondary (Industry), Tertiary (Services).
- GDP = Gross Domestic Product.
- GNP = Gross National Product.
- Per capita income = National income / Population.

## Unit 3: NITI Aayog

- Established: 2015.

## Unit 4: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

- Established: 1935.
- Nationalised: 1949.
- First Governor: Osborne Smith.
- Key functions: Currency issue, Monetary policy, Bank supervision.
- Key tools: Repo, Reverse repo, CRR, SLR, MSF, Bank rate.

## Unit 6: Monetary Policy

- Objective: Inflation control, Money supply.
- Tools: Repo, Reverse repo, CRR, SLR, OMO.
- Target: 4% CPI inflation (±2%).

## Unit 7: Fiscal Policy

- Concepts: Fiscal deficit, Revenue deficit, Primary deficit.

Unit 11: National Income
National Income = Sum of all factor incomes.

GDP = Market value of all final goods and services produced within a country.

GNP = GDP + Net factor income from abroad.

NNP = GNP – Depreciation.

Personal income = Income received by individuals.

Disposable income = Personal income – Direct taxes.

Unit 12: Inflation
Inflation = Sustained rise in general price level.

Types: Demand-pull, Cost-push, Built-in.

Measurement: CPI, WPI, GDP deflator.

Effects: Reduces purchasing power, affects savings.

Control: Monetary policy, Fiscal policy.
