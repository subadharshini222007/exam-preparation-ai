
# 👑 The Three Kingdoms

---

# 🟤 1. INTRODUCTION TO THREE KINGDOMS

The Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas were the three major Tamil kingdoms of ancient Sangam Age (300 BCE – 300 CE). These kingdoms collectively formed Tamilakam, which was known for its rich culture, trade, literature, and political stability.

These three kingdoms frequently fought among themselves but also contributed equally to Tamil language development and South Indian history.

---

# 🟢 2. CHERAS KINGDOM

---

## 2.1 INTRODUCTION

The Chera dynasty was one of the oldest Tamil dynasties. They ruled parts of present-day Kerala and western Tamil Nadu. Cheras were famous for spice trade, maritime power, and elephants.

---

## 2.2 CAPITALS

* Vanji (Karur)
* Later: Makotai (Mahodayapuram)

---

## 2.3 SYMBOL

* Bow and Arrow

---

## 2.4 IMPORTANT CHERA RULERS

### Senguttuvan (Red Chera)

* Most famous Chera king
* Known as “Kadal Pirakottiya Vel Kezhu Kuttuvan”
* Linked with Kannagi legend (Silappathikaram)

### Udiyan Cheralathan

* Early Chera ruler
* Known for food distribution and generosity

### Nedum Cheralathan

* Expanded Chera territory
* Fought many battles with Cholas and Pandyas

---

## 2.5 ECONOMY OF CHERAS

* Spice trade (pepper, cardamom)
* International trade with Romans
* Port cities played key role

---

## 2.6 IMPORTANT PORTS

* Muziris (very important international port)
* Tondi

---

## 2.7 CULTURE AND SOCIETY

* Strong Tamil culture
* Patronized Sangam poets
* Women had respectable position
* Martial tradition strong

---

## 2.8 KEY FACTS (CHERAS)

✔ Famous for spice trade
✔ Capital – Vanji
✔ Emblem – Bow
✔ Muziris port
✔ Senguttuvan linked to Silappathikaram

---

# 🟡 3. CHOLAS KINGDOM

---

## 3.1 INTRODUCTION

The Cholas were one of the most powerful dynasties of South India. They were known for strong administration, naval power, irrigation systems, and temple architecture.

---

## 3.2 CAPITALS

* Uraiyur (early capital)
* Later: Puhar (Kaveripattinam)

---

## 3.3 SYMBOL

* Tiger

---

## 3.4 IMPORTANT CHOLA RULERS

### Karikala Chola (Most famous)

* Built Kallanai (Grand Anicut dam) on Kaveri river
* Strong military ruler
* Expanded Chola kingdom

### Ilamcetcenni

* Early Chola king
* Known for valor

### Nedunkilli & Nalankilli

* Mentioned in Sangam literature
* Internal conflicts existed

---

## 3.5 ADMINISTRATION

* Village self-government system
* Strong local administration
* Efficient tax system

---

## 3.6 IRRIGATION SYSTEM

* Kallanai dam built by Karikala Chola
* One of the oldest water regulation structures in the world

---

## 3.7 ECONOMY OF CHOLAS

* Agriculture (rice cultivation)
* Inland trade
* Sea trade via ports

---

## 3.8 IMPORTANT PORTS

* Puhar (Kaveripattinam) – major Chola port
* Arikamedu (trade center with Romans)

---

## 3.9 CULTURE

* Strong Tamil literature support
* Early temple development
* Music and dance culture

---

## 3.10 KEY FACTS (CHOLAS)

✔ Emblem – Tiger
✔ Capital – Uraiyur / Puhar
✔ Karikala built Kallanai
✔ Strong naval power
✔ Advanced irrigation system

---

# 🔵 4. PANDYAS KINGDOM

---

## 4.1 INTRODUCTION

The Pandya dynasty was one of the oldest Tamil kingdoms, known for pearl trade, Tamil Sangam patronage, and Madurai as cultural capital.

---

## 4.2 CAPITAL

* Madurai (cultural capital of Tamil Nadu)

---

## 4.3 SYMBOL

* Fish

---

## 4.4 IMPORTANT PANDYA RULERS

### Nedunchezhiyan

* Famous Pandya king
* Mentioned in Silappathikaram story

### Nedumaran

* Supported Tamil culture
* Known for administration

### Palyagasalai Mudukudumi Peruvazhuthi

* Early Pandya ruler
* Performed Vedic sacrifices

---

## 4.5 ECONOMY OF PANDYAS

* Pearl fishing (very famous)
* Trade with foreign countries
* Agriculture in fertile regions

---

## 4.6 PORTS

* Korkai (famous pearl port)
* Alagankulam

---

## 4.7 CULTURE

* Strong Tamil literature support
* Madurai was Sangam capital
* Temple culture development

---

## 4.8 RELIGION

* Murugan worship
* Jainism and Buddhism influence
* Later Hindu temple growth

---

## 4.9 KEY FACTS (PANDYAS)

✔ Capital – Madurai
✔ Emblem – Fish
✔ Famous for pearl trade
✔ Korkai port
✔ Sangam patronage

# 📜 6. IMPORTANT TERMS

* Tamilakam – ancient Tamil region
* Sangam – assembly of poets
* Muziris – Chera port
* Kallanai – Chola dam
* Korkai – Pandya pearl port
* Yavana – foreigners (Greeks/Romans)

# 📌 9. SUMMARY

The Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas were the backbone of ancient Tamil civilization. They contributed to trade, culture, irrigation, literature, and political development. Their legacy is still visible in Tamil Nadu’s culture and history.
