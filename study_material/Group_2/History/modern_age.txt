

#  MODERN AGE – INDIAN HISTORY

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION TO MODERN INDIAN HISTORY

Modern Indian History generally refers to the period from the arrival of Europeans in India to Indian Independence in 1947. This period includes the establishment of British rule, major revolts, social reform movements, the freedom struggle, and the final independence of India.

This is one of the most important units in TNPSC exams because many questions are asked from freedom struggle leaders, acts passed by British government, and important movements like Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, and Quit India Movement.

---

## 2. ARRIVAL OF EUROPEANS IN INDIA

The arrival of Europeans marked the beginning of modern Indian history.

### Major European Powers:

* Portuguese
* Dutch
* English (British)
* French
* Danish

---

### 2.1 PORTUGUESE

* First Europeans to arrive in India
* Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in 1498
* Established trade monopoly in coastal areas

### Important Portuguese settlements:

* Goa
* Diu
* Daman

---

### 2.2 DUTCH

* Came for spice trade
* Established factories in Surat, Pulicat
* Later lost power to British

---

### 2.3 FRENCH

* Established Pondicherry
* Rivalry with British (Carnatic Wars)
* Eventually defeated by British

---

### 2.4 BRITISH EAST INDIA COMPANY

* Founded in 1600 by Queen Elizabeth I
* Established trading posts in Surat, Madras, Bombay and Calcutta
* Slowly became political power in India

---

## 3. BRITISH EXPANSION IN INDIA

British expansion happened through wars, treaties and policies.

### Important Battles:

* Battle of Plassey (1757) – Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah
* Battle of Buxar (1764) – British gained Bengal control

After Buxar, British became the dominant political power in India.

---

## 4. GOVERNOR GENERALS AND VICEROYS

### Important Governor Generals:

#### Warren Hastings

* First Governor-General of India
* Established administrative system

#### Lord Cornwallis

* Introduced Permanent Settlement
* Civil services reforms

#### Lord Wellesley

* Subsidiary Alliance system

#### Lord Dalhousie

* Doctrine of Lapse
* Introduced railways and telegraph

---

## 5. BRITISH ADMINISTRATIVE POLICIES

### Key Systems:

* Permanent Settlement (Zamindari system)
* Ryotwari system
* Mahalwari system

These systems heavily exploited Indian farmers.

---

## 6. 1857 REVOLT (FIRST WAR OF INDEPENDENCE)

The Revolt of 1857 was the first major uprising against British rule.

### Causes:

* Economic exploitation
* Social and religious interference
* Enfield rifle cartridges issue
* Disrespect to Indian soldiers

### Leaders:

* Rani Lakshmibai
* Mangal Pandey
* Nana Sahib
* Tantia Tope
* Bahadur Shah Zafar

### Outcome:

* Revolt failed
* End of East India Company rule
* British Crown took direct control (1858)

---

## 7. SOCIAL AND RELIGIOUS REFORM MOVEMENTS

During British rule, several reform movements started to remove social evils.

---

### 7.1 RAJA RAM MOHAN ROY

* Founder of Brahmo Samaj
* Opposed Sati system
* Promoted modern education

---

### 7.2 SWAMI VIVEKANANDA

* Founder of Ramakrishna Mission
* Speech at Chicago Parliament (1893)
* Promoted Indian spirituality

---

### 7.3 DAYANAND SARASWATI

* Founder of Arya Samaj
* “Back to Vedas” ideology

---

### 7.4 ISLAMIC REFORM MOVEMENTS

* Sir Syed Ahmed Khan
* Founded Aligarh Movement

---

## 8. INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS (INC)

Founded in 1885 by A.O. Hume.

### Objectives:

* Political rights for Indians
* Representation in administration
* Unity among Indians

---

## 9. MODERATE PHASE (1885–1905)

### Leaders:

* Dadabhai Naoroji
* Gopal Krishna Gokhale
* Surendranath Banerjee

### Methods:

* Petitions
* Peaceful protests
* Constitutional reforms

---

## 10. EXTREMIST PHASE (1905–1919)

### Leaders:

* Bal Gangadhar Tilak
* Bipin Chandra Pal
* Lala Lajpat Rai

### Methods:

* Swadeshi movement
* Boycott of British goods
* National education

---

## 11. PARTITION OF BENGAL (1905)

* Lord Curzon divided Bengal
* Led to Swadeshi Movement
* Protest against British policy

---

## 12. GANDHIAN ERA (1915–1947)

Mahatma Gandhi led India’s freedom struggle using non-violence (Ahimsa) and Satyagraha.

---

## 13. IMPORTANT MOVEMENTS

### 13.1 NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT (1920)

* Led by Gandhi
* Boycott of British goods, schools, courts

---

### 13.2 CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MOVEMENT (1930)

* Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha)
* Breaking Salt Law

---

### 13.3 QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT (1942)

* “Do or Die” slogan
* Mass protest against British rule

---

## 14. IMPORTANT NATIONAL LEADERS

### Mahatma Gandhi

* Father of Nation
* Non-violence leader

### Jawaharlal Nehru

* First Prime Minister of India

### Subhas Chandra Bose

* Founded INA (Indian National Army)
* “Give me blood, I will give you freedom”

### Bhagat Singh

* Revolutionary freedom fighter
* Lahore conspiracy case

### Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

* Iron Man of India
* Integration of princely states

---

## 15. INDIAN NATIONAL ARMY (INA)

* Founded by Subhas Chandra Bose
* Aimed to fight British with Japanese support
* “Azad Hind Fauj”

---

## 16. IMPORTANT LAWS AND ACTS

* Regulating Act (1773)
* Pitt’s India Act (1784)
* Charter Acts (1813, 1833, 1853)
* Government of India Act (1935)
* Indian Independence Act (1947)

---

## 17. IMPORTANT EVENTS TIMELINE

* 1498 – Vasco da Gama arrives
* 1757 – Battle of Plassey
* 1857 – Revolt
* 1885 – INC formed
* 1905 – Bengal Partition
* 1920 – Non-Cooperation
* 1930 – Salt March
* 1942 – Quit India Movement
* 1947 – Independence

---

## 18. IMPORTANT TERMS

* Satyagraha – truth force
* Swadeshi – self-reliance
* Doctrine of Lapse – annexation policy
* Subsidiary Alliance – British control system
* Zamindari system – land tax system
