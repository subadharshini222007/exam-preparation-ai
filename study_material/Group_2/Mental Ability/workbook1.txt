PART A – LOGICAL REASONING
==========================

Q1. Statements: All administrators are graduates. All graduates are educated. Which conclusion follows?

A) All administrators are educated
B) All educated people are administrators
C) No administrators are educated
D) Some graduates are not educated

Answer: A

Q2. Statements: Some teachers are writers. All writers are readers. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

A) All teachers are readers
B) Some teachers are readers
C) No teachers are readers
D) All readers are teachers

Answer: B

Q3. Statements: No reptiles are warm-blooded. All snakes are reptiles. Which conclusion follows?

A) All snakes are warm-blooded
B) Some snakes are warm-blooded
C) No snakes are warm-blooded
D) All reptiles are snakes

Answer: C

Q4. Statements: All engineers are problem-solvers. Some problem-solvers are artists. Which conclusion is definitely valid?

A) Some engineers are artists
B) All artists are engineers
C) All engineers are problem-solvers
D) No artists are problem-solvers

Answer: C

Q5. Statements: Some rivers are polluted. No polluted river is safe for drinking without treatment. Which conclusion follows?

A) Some rivers are not safe for drinking without treatment
B) All rivers are polluted
C) No river can be treated
D) All safe rivers are polluted

Answer: A

Q6. Statement: The municipal corporation plans to increase public dustbins. Which assumption is most reasonable?

A) More dustbins may make proper waste disposal easier
B) Dustbins eliminate all pollution
C) People never use dustbins
D) All waste is recyclable

Answer: A

Q7. Statement: A school introduces extra practice sessions before examinations. Which assumption is most reasonable?

A) Extra practice may help students prepare better
B) All students have already passed
C) Practice cannot improve performance
D) Examinations will be cancelled

Answer: A

Q8. Statement: A city introduces additional buses during peak hours. Which assumption is most reasonable?

A) Passenger demand may be high during peak hours
B) All passengers travel only at night
C) Additional buses reduce road length
D) Every bus will be empty

Answer: A

Q9. Assertion (A): Metals generally conduct electricity.
Reason (R): Metals contain mobile electrons that can carry electric charge.

Choose the correct answer.

A) Both A and R are true, and R explains A
B) Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A
C) A is true, but R is false
D) A is false, but R is true

Answer: A

Q10. Assertion (A): The Earth revolves around the Sun.
Reason (R): The Earth rotates on its axis approximately once every 24 hours.

Choose the correct answer.

A) Both A and R are true, and R explains A
B) Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A
C) A is true, but R is false
D) A is false, but R is true

Answer: B

Q11. Argument: Should schools teach road safety?

Argument I: It can help students learn safer road practices.
Argument II: It guarantees that road accidents will never occur.

A) Only I is strong
B) Only II is strong
C) Both I and II are strong
D) Neither I nor II is strong

Answer: A

Q12. Situation: Several residents report that drinking water may be contaminated.

Course of action I: Test the water and investigate the source.
Course of action II: Ignore the complaints.

A) Only I is appropriate
B) Only II is appropriate
C) Both are appropriate
D) Neither is appropriate

Answer: A

Q13. If all registered candidates received admit cards, which statement is definitely true?

A) Every person with an admit card was registered
B) Every registered candidate received an admit card
C) No unregistered person received an admit card
D) Every registered candidate attended the examination

Answer: B

Q14. Statements: All A are B. No B are C. Which conclusion follows?

A) Some A are C
B) No A are C
C) All C are A
D) Some B are C

Answer: B

Q15. Statements: Some cars are electric vehicles. All electric vehicles use electric motors. Which conclusion follows?

A) Some cars use electric motors
B) All cars use electric motors
C) No cars use electric motors
D) All electric motors are cars

Answer: A
