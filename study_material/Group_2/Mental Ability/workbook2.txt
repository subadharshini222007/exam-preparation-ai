
# LOGICAL REASONING AND ANALYTICAL REASONING

## PART A: LOGICAL REASONING

### Question 1: Statements and Conclusions

Statements:
All teachers are graduates.
All graduates are educated.

Which conclusion follows?

A) All educated people are teachers
B) All teachers are educated
C) No teachers are educated
D) Some graduates are not educated

Answer: B) All teachers are educated

### Question 2: Syllogism

Statements:
All mangoes are fruits.
All fruits are edible.

Conclusion:

A) All mangoes are edible
B) All edible things are mangoes
C) No mangoes are edible
D) Some mangoes are not fruits

Answer: A) All mangoes are edible

### Question 3: Statement and Assumption

Statement: The government advises people to conserve water.

Which assumption is implicit?

A) Water is available in unlimited quantities
B) Water conservation is important
C) People should stop using water
D) Rainfall occurs every day

Answer: B) Water conservation is important

### Question 4: Assertion and Reason

Assertion: Plants need sunlight for photosynthesis.

Reason: Sunlight provides energy for photosynthesis.

A) Both are true, and the reason explains the assertion
B) Both are true, but the reason does not explain the assertion
C) Assertion is true, but reason is false
D) Assertion is false, but reason is true

Answer: A) Both are true, and the reason explains the assertion

### Question 5: Strong Argument

Question: Should schools conduct regular fire-safety drills?

Argument I: Fire-safety drills help students practise emergency evacuation.

Argument II: Some students do not like drills.

A) Only Argument I is strong
B) Only Argument II is strong
C) Both arguments are strong
D) Neither argument is strong

Answer: A) Only Argument I is strong

### Question 6: Cause and Effect

Event I: Heavy rainfall occurred continuously for several hours.

Event II: Low-lying streets became flooded.

What is the most likely relationship?

A) Event II causes Event I
B) Both events are unrelated
C) Event I may cause Event II
D) Event I must be caused by Event II

Answer: C) Event I may cause Event II

### Question 7: Course of Action

Problem: Road accidents are increasing near a school because vehicles travel too fast.

Which action is most appropriate?

A) Close the school permanently
B) Ignore the problem
C) Remove traffic signs
D) Introduce suitable speed controls and traffic monitoring

Answer: D) Introduce suitable speed controls and traffic monitoring

### Question 8: Logical Deduction

Statements:
All engineers are graduates.
Ravi is an engineer.

Which conclusion follows?

A) Ravi is a graduate
B) Ravi is a doctor
C) All graduates are engineers
D) Ravi is not educated

Answer: A) Ravi is a graduate

### Question 9: Statements and Conclusions

Conclusion I: Some books are interesting.
Conclusion II: All books are interesting.

A) Only Conclusion I follows
B) Only Conclusion II follows
C) Both conclusions follow
D) Neither conclusion follows

Answer: A) Only Conclusion I follows

### Question 10: Syllogism

No birds are mammals.
All sparrows are birds.

Conclusion:

A) All mammals are sparrows
B) No sparrows are mammals
C) All sparrows are mammals
D) Some mammals are birds

Answer: B) No sparrows are mammals

### Question 11: Statement and Assumption

Statement: A college introduces extra practice sessions before examinations.

Which assumption is most reasonable?

A) Students never attend regular classes
B) Examinations are unnecessary
C) Additional practice may help students prepare
D) All students have already passed

Answer: C) Additional practice may help students prepare

### Question 12: Strong and Weak Arguments

Question: Should public places provide drinking-water facilities?

Argument I: Such facilities can help people access drinking water while outside.

Argument II: Public places have different wall colours.

A) Only Argument I is strong
B) Only Argument II is strong
C) Both are strong
D) Neither is strong

Answer: A) Only Argument I is strong

### Question 13: Logical Deduction

Statements:
All doctors are professionals.
Some professionals are writers.

Which conclusion definitely follows?

A) All writers are doctors
B) Some doctors are writers
C) No professionals are doctors
D) All doctors are professionals

Answer: D) All doctors are professionals

### Question 14: Course of Action

Problem: A village faces frequent drinking-water shortages during summer.

Which action is most appropriate?

A) Waste available water
B) Repair leaking pipes and improve water-storage arrangements
C) Stop maintaining water sources
D) Ignore the shortage

Answer: B) Repair leaking pipes and improve water-storage arrangements

### Question 15: Cause and Effect

Event I: A vehicle's engine overheats because its cooling system fails.

Event II: The vehicle stops working.

Which relationship is most reasonable?

A) Event II always causes Event I
B) Both events must be unrelated
C) Event I may cause Event II
D) Event I is the result of Event II in every case

Answer: C) Event I may cause Event II

### Question 16: Statement and Conclusion

Statement: Every applicant must submit the required documents before the deadline.

Conclusion I: Applicants should submit the documents on time.
Conclusion II: Applicants can always submit documents after the deadline.

A) Only Conclusion I follows
B) Only Conclusion II follows
C) Both conclusions follow
D) Neither conclusion follows

Answer: A) Only Conclusion I follows

### Question 17: Assertion and Reason

Argument II: Every city has buildings.

A) Only Argument I is strong
B) Only Argument II is strong

### Question 20: Logical Deduction

Some students are athletes.

A) All students are athletes
